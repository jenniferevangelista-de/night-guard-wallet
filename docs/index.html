🌙 Night Guard Wallet

A Solana wallet guard for our parents, grandparents and the rest of us. It simulates every transaction, checks where every part comes from, explains it in plain words with local AI, locks risky payments behind a family guardian on-chain, and turns every refused scam into a warning for every family.

Why

In 2025, people over 60 reported $7.7 billion lost to fraud to the FBI, $4.3 billion of it involving crypto, $38,500 per victim on average (FBI IC3 2025 Internet Crime Report). On a blockchain there is no bank to call: one signature can't be undone.
